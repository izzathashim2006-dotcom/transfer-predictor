Data-Driven Transfer Prediction

The Transfer Predictor estimates the probability and fee of every professional footballer's next move — before it happens.

Two gradient-boosted machine-learning models are trained on {{ metrics.n_rows|default("56,204") }} real player-seasons (2013–2024) built from Transfermarkt data — and it scores the current 2025/26 season as it happens.

Each player is scored on on-pitch performance (goals, assists, minutes, per-90 rates), age curve (peak odds at ~24–25) and injury history (appearance gaps, availability ratio), alongside contract context and 12-month valuation trends.
