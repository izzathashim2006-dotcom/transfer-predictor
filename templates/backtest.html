Honest scoreboard

The model confidently called 5 of the 11 most expensive moves — including Bellingham (95%), Núñez (94%), Antony (93%), Gvardiol (92%) and Caicedo (91%). It also missed the genuine surprises: nobody sane had Rice at 11% or Lukaku at 4%, because big-club interest simply isn't visible in match data. That's the honest boundary of what statistics can know — and exactly why the live rumour feed exists next to the model.
